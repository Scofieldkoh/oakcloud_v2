1. **Critical — optimistic concurrency and truthful APPLY counts** — **PARTIALLY ADDRESSED.** The destructive race is fixed: every write now reasserts the stored identity, `updatedAt`, `OPEN`, future-date, and both override flags, and APPLY increments `recalculated`/`cancelled` only from `updateMany.count` (`src/services/billing/reconciler.ts:263-290`, `:555-575`, `:602-617`).
